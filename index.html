HMS fair-payment purchasing assessment

1. The piece

Who made it and what it is.

A sample and its production batch share one SKU. Pick the existing SKU when you come back to order.
One primary category per piece. Material is not a category.

2. What the maker asks

Always ask this. It is the starting point, and you never pay less than it.

Recorded for context only. Does not set the price and does not reject a piece.

3. What the work costs them

Ask while they are working, not while you are negotiating. Ranges are fine. This verifies the price, it does not replace it.

Fuel, tool wear, rent, wastage, help.

4. The payment

The higher of their price and the production floor. Paid in full on the day.
